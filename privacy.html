Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026. This describes the Android app Castle Defense Cuh (package ID com.castledefense.cuh) and this website.

The campaign itself — placing towers, fighting waves, and saving a run — is played on your device. The app also includes some optional or background online features. This page describes those based on the current app code, not on guesses.

What stays on your device

  • Gameplay, maps, and combat run locally.
  • Saved runs, settings (such as sound, music, vibration, and theme), and a local copy of scores can be stored on the device (including in the app’s local storage or app files).
  • You do not need an account to play the campaign.

Internet permission

The app requests the Android Internet permission. That is used for the optional and background features below, not for playing a level.

Optional rewarded ads (Google AdMob)

After a defeat, you can choose to watch a rewarded ad to revive. The app includes Google AdMob for this. If you watch an ad, Google may collect data as described in Google’s privacy policy and AdMob’s guidance. Playing the game does not require you to watch an ad.

Analytics and crash reports (Firebase)

The app includes Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. These services can collect technical information such as app version, device and OS details, and crash logs, and usage events used to understand stability and how the app is used. Google describes this in the Google privacy policy and Firebase documentation.

Optional leaderboard (Firebase)

If you submit a score or open the global leaderboard, the app uses Firebase Authentication (anonymous sign-in, so a random ID rather than your Google email) and Firebase Realtime Database. A submitted entry can include a name you type, the number of rounds, a time in seconds, and a timestamp. You can skip score submission and keep playing.

Optional bug reports

If you send a bug report from the app, the report is submitted to a third-party form service (Formspree) so it can be read by the developer. Only send information you are comfortable sharing in that report.

Vibration

The app may use the device vibrator for haptic feedback. This can be turned off in settings. The app does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, or precise location.

Android backups

The app allows Android backup. Depending on your device settings, app data such as a saved run may be included in a backup associated with your Google account.

This website

This promotional website is a static site. It does not use analytics, advertising, social pixels, or tracking cookies. Links to Google Play and to Google’s policies are ordinary links to those sites, which have their own privacy practices.

Children

The game is a general-audience tower defense game. The online features above (ads, analytics, crash reporting, leaderboard, and bug reports) may involve third parties that collect data. If you are a parent or guardian, review those services before a child uses the optional online features.

Changes

If the app’s network, advertising, or analytics behavior changes, this policy should be updated to match. The date at the top of the page is the last update.

Contact

There is no separate support inbox published on this site. You can use the in-app bug report, or the Google Play listing for the app when it is available to you.

This page is a plain-language description of what the current app code does. It is not legal advice.
