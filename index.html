Castle Defense Cuh

Battle for the Castle.

Build a maze, place your towers, and hold the line as enemies pour from the portal. A campaign of 25 levels, a roster of towers and traps, and a boss waiting later in the run.

The game

You build the path. They try to break it.

Enemies spawn at the portal and walk the maze you create. Spend gold on walls, traps, and towers, upgrade what works, and keep the castle standing through each round.

Maze building

Place walls and structures to shape the route. The path to the castle is yours to design.

A full tower roster

Machine guns, cannons, snipers, AA, mortars, Tesla coils, frost, lasers, flame throwers, and an Upgrader that boosts neighbors.

25 levels, then a boss

World 1: Outskirts runs through 25 levels. Mid-campaign, a boss level throws heavier waves at your defense.

Towers & buildables

MG

Rapid fire. Can shoot anything, but struggles against Tanks and often misses Flyers.

Cannon

Heavy ground shots. Slow to turn, with a blind spot up close.

Sniper

Long-range ground fire, with a donut blind spot near the tower.

AA

Homing rockets that only hit Flyers.

Mortar

Arcing splash damage on ground units, also with a close-range gap.

Tesla

Shocks every enemy in range, including Flyers. No projectiles.

Upgrader

Does not shoot. Periodically boosts adjacent towers.

Frost Tower

Slows ground enemies in an area around each shot.

Laser Tower

A continuous ground beam you can rotate, and bounce with Mirrors.

Flame Thrower

A short, rotatable cone of fire against ground enemies.

Wall

Cheap, sturdy, and the backbone of your maze.

Mirror

A rotatable reflector for laser beams.

Spike Trap

A path trap that damages and slows enemies as they walk over it. Upgrades through several tiers.

Lilypad

Placed on water. Slows, and can damage, enemies that cross it.

Enemy types

Normies

Standard ground troops. The baseline of a wave.

Groupies

Come in packs rather than one at a time.

Speedies

Fast. Easy to lose if your maze is short or your guns are slow.

Tanks

Slow, tough, and hard for machine guns to chew through.

Crocodiles

Can swim, so water is not a barrier for them.

Flyers

Fly over the maze. AA is built for them; many ground towers cannot help.

Splitters

Split into smaller units when they go down.

Breakers

Heavy, spinning units whose aura damages nearby towers and can interrupt upgrades.

Boss rounds also appear in the campaign — including a dedicated boss level — with tougher, larger enemies.
